📘 How to Play

1. Start
Enter your name, then press Start New Quest.
2. Answer
Choose the correct answer from four choices.
3. Earn
Correct answer = 100 points + 1 coin.
4. Lives
Wrong answer costs 1 life. The quest ends when lives reach zero.
5. Progress
Your runner moves toward the finish flag after each correct answer.
6. Win
Complete 10 levels. Your score is saved to the leaderboard on this browser.

Curriculum Coverage

Questions are drawn from Year 5 KSSR Mathematics strands: whole numbers and operations, fractions/decimals/percentages, money, time, measurement, space, coordinates/ratio, and data handling.
